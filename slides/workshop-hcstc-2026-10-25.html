美国推荐筛查时间 U.S. SCREENING TIMELINE
美国儿科学会 (AAP) / CDC 建议 American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP) / CDC guidance
  • 每次体检：发育监测（developmental monitoring / surveillance） Developmental monitoring (surveillance) at every well-child visit
  • 至少在 9、18、30 个月：标准化发育筛查 Standardized developmental screening at least at 9, 18, and 30 months
  • 18 与 24 个月：专门的自闭症筛查（如 M-CHAT-R/F；CDC 不特别认可任何单一工具） Autism-specific screening at 18 and 24 months (e.g., M-CHAT-R/F; CDC does not endorse any single tool)
  • 任何年龄：若发现技能丧失或倒退，请尽快联系儿科医生，不要等到下次体检 Any age: contact your pediatrician promptly if skills are lost or regress; don't wait for the next visit
  • 另外：若对发育有任何担忧，可随时要求额外筛查；CDC 里程碑清单不能取代正式筛查 Also: if you have any developmental concern, you can ask for extra screening at any time; CDC milestone checklists do not replace formal screening
识别早期迹象 · 社交 EARLY SIGNS · SOCIAL
  • 喂食或玩耍时眼神交流有限或短暂 Limited or fleeting eye contact during feeding or play
  • 叫名字时没有反应（CDC 里程碑：约 9 个月） Not responding to name (CDC milestone: by about 9 months)
  • 家长指向物体时不跟着看 Not following when a parent points to an object
  • 很少或没有假想游戏（CDC 里程碑：约 30 个月） Little or no pretend play (CDC milestone: by about 30 months)

里程碑年龄依据 CDC 2022 修订版；每个孩子发育节奏不同，有担忧请咨询儿科医生。 Milestone ages follow CDC's 2022 revised checklists; children develop at different rates. If you have concerns, talk to your pediatrician.

识别早期迹象 · 沟通与行为 EARLY SIGNS · COMMUNICATION & BEHAVIOR
  • 12 个月不会咿呀学语，或 16–18 个月不会说单个词 No babbling by 12 months, or no single words by 16–18 months
  • 12 个月不会挥手再见；15 个月不会用手指要东西；18 个月不会用手指给你看 Not waving bye-bye by 12 months; not pointing to ask by 15 months; not pointing to show by 18 months
  • 重复动作：拍手、摇晃、旋转物体 Repetitive motions: hand-flapping, rocking, spinning objects
  • 对日常声音或光线极度敏感 Extreme distress with everyday sounds or lights
黄金法则（任何年龄）： Golden rule (any age): 任何言语、眼神、手势或社交能力的倒退，请尽快咨询儿科医生。 Any regression in speech, eye contact, gestures, or social skills needs prompt evaluation by a pediatrician.
居家观察要点 WHAT TO WATCH FOR AT HOME
留意这 4 件事 Four things to watch for
当你和孩子说话/玩耍时，他会看你吗？ Looks at you when you speak/play?
如果你指向玩具，孩子会看向玩具吗？ Looks at a toy when you point?
孩子会指给你看有趣的东西吗？ Points to show you something interesting?
当你对孩子微笑时，他会回以微笑吗？ Smiles back when you smile?
如果对其中任何一项不确定，或有其他担忧，请咨询儿科医生，并询问发育与自闭症筛查（如 M-CHAT-R/F）。这份清单不是筛查工具。 If you are unsure about any of these, or have other concerns, talk to your pediatrician and ask about developmental and autism screening (e.g., M-CHAT-R/F). This list is not a screening tool.

寻求支持 4 步骤 4 STEPS TO SEEK SUPPORT
  • 1 观察与记录：记下具体担忧，对照里程碑清单。 Observe & track concerns with milestone checklists.
  • 2 咨询儿科医生：在体检时主动要求 M-CHAT-R/F 筛查。 Ask your pediatrician for an M-CHAT-R/F screen.
  • 3 综合评估：中高风险时转介发育儿科或早期干预团队。 Moderate/high risk prompts referral for full evaluation.
  • 4 启动早期干预：联系 Fairfax ITC（0–3 岁）或 FCPS Child Find（20 个月–5 岁）。怀疑有迟缓即可转介，无需正式诊断；转介无需 SSN。 Contact Fairfax ITC (0–3) or FCPS Child Find (20 mo–5 yr). A suspected delay is enough to refer; no formal diagnosis needed, and no SSN needed to make a referral.
