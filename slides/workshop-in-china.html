识别早期迹象 · 2岁以下 RECOGNIZING EARLY SIGNS
社交和视觉线索 Social & Visual Cues
  • 喂食或玩耍时眼神交流有限或短暂 Limited or fleeting eye contact during feeding or play
  • 叫名字时没有反应（12个月前） Not responding to name when called (by 12 months)
  • 家长指向物体时不跟着看 Not following when a parent points to an object
识别早期迹象（续） RECOGNIZING EARLY SIGNS (CONTINUED)
沟通和行为 Communication & Behavior
  • 12个月时不会咿呀学语，或16–18个月时不会说单个词 Lack of babbling by 12 months, or no single words by 16–18 months
  • 12–14个月时不会挥手再见、用手指表达需求或拍手 Not waving goodbye, pointing, or clapping by 12–14 months
  • 重复动作：拍手、摇晃、旋转物体 Repetitive motions: hand-flapping, rocking, spinning objects
居家简单筛查 SIMPLE HOME SCREENING
问自己4个简单问题 Ask Yourself These 4 Simple Questions
当你和孩子说话/玩耍时，他会看你吗？ Looks at you when you speak/play?
如果你指向玩具，孩子会看向玩具吗？ Looks at toy when you point?
孩子会指给你看有趣的东西吗？ Points to show you something interesting?
当你对孩子微笑时，他会回以微笑吗？ Smiles back when you smile?
如果2个或更多问题回答"否"，请咨询医生。 If you answered "NO" to 2 or more questions, talk to your doctor.
