What Your Score Means

Your child is in the Low Risk category. This indicates a low probability of developmental risk or autism.

No formal follow-up is recommended at this time unless developmental concerns persist. If your child is currently younger than 24 months, it is recommended to screen them again after their second birthday.

Your child is in the Medium Risk category. This indicates a moderate risk of autism or developmental delay.

The recommended clinical next step for a score of 3–7 is to administer the **M-CHAT-R Follow-Up interview** (a second stage of screening). This interview helps clarify responses that may be false alarms. We highly recommend saving or printing this report and discussing the findings with your child's pediatrician.

Your child is in the High Risk category. This indicates an elevated likelihood of autism.

It is recommended to bypass the follow-up interview and immediately refer your child to a developmental pediatrician or psychologist for a comprehensive diagnostic evaluation, as well as checking eligibility for early intervention services. Early screening and support make a significant positive impact on childhood development.

Important Disclaimer: The M-CHAT-R is a preliminary screening tool, not a diagnostic test. A positive screen only indicates the need for further professional assessment. If you have concerns, always contact a healthcare provider regardless of this score.
