Early signs to watch for

  • No big smiles or joyful expressions by 6 months
  • No back-and-forth sharing of sounds, smiles, or facial expressions by 9 months
  • No babbling by 12 months; loss of language or social skills at any age
  • Poor eye contact, limited pointing or gestures

Screening & Early Intervention

Free screening programs (ChildFind), pediatric developmental screenings, and local early intervention services can evaluate concerns — earlier is better. If you have concerns, ask your pediatrician or contact ChildFind.

China-specific resources (sample)

For rural families in Hubei, local clinic contacts and provincial child health centers are the best first step. Materials distributed in clinics include Mandarin pamphlets with local program contacts.
