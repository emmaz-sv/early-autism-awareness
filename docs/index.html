Project overview

This site documents Emma Zheng’s Girl Scout Gold Award project to increase early autism awareness among Chinese-speaking families in the DMV area and rural Hubei, China. Early diagnosis (18–36 months) greatly improves long-term outcomes; the site provides simple checklists, screening resources, downloadable bilingual pamphlets, and a public project journal.

  • Goal: Reach local immigrant families and rural families in Hubei with clear bilingual information.
  • Targets: 35 local parents, 15 in-person attendees in China, 15 families engaged in clinics.
  • Outputs: Reusable pamphlet templates, bilingual website, recorded outreach events.
