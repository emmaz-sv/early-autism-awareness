Who We Are

Empowering Families through Early Action

This project documents Emma Zheng’s Girl Scout Gold Award initiative to increase early autism awareness among Chinese-speaking families in the DMV area and rural Hubei, China.

Early diagnosis (between 18–36 months) is the single most critical factor in improving long-term developmental outcomes. Due to language barriers and cultural stigmas, many immigrant and rural families experience significant delays in accessing developmental care. We bridge this gap by providing clear bilingual checklists, screening directions, and printable guides.

Key Project Benchmarks

  • Core Goal: Overcome cultural stigma and language barriers to reach local immigrant parents and rural families in Hubei with evidence-based early screening guidance.
  • Target Footprint: 35+ local parents through Chinese school presentations and church distribution, 15+ seminar attendees in China, and 15+ clinic family engagements.
  • Outputs: 500 printed bilingual brochures (funded by cookie proceeds), reusable open-source templates, an interactive online screener, and seminar logs.
  • Long-Term Sustainability: The web hub remains permanently open; pamphlet files are freely downloadable for community printing; church and rural clinic partners maintain active restocking.
