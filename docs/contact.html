Official resources

Authoritative public resources for information, screening guidance, and early intervention:

  • CDC — Autism Spectrum Disorder — signs, screening guidance, and family resources.
  • NIH / NICHD — Autism & Early Intervention — research-backed information on early intervention.
  • World Health Organization — Autism Spectrum Disorders — global information and guidance.
  • U.S. Department of Education — IDEA (Early Intervention) — Individuals with Disabilities Education Act resources for infants and toddlers.
  • ChildFind (Virginia) — local screening and referral resources (check your state for local ChildFind programs).
  • American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP) — guidance on developmental screening and pediatric practice.
