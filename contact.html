Presentations

To request a presentation at a Chinese school or community group, email the project lead: ezhengva@gmail.com

Primary Venue

  • Hope Chinese School — Tyson's Corner Campus (HCS-TC) — Main venue. Closest campus to Vienna. Classes meet Saturday or Sunday, 1–6 pm; parent break period (roughly 2–4 pm) is ideal for presentations. hopechineseschool.org/hcstc

Secondary Venue

  • CCCVA (北维州华人福音教会) — 541 Marshall Rd. SW, Vienna, VA 22180 — Chinese church services in the Vienna area. Presentations can be arranged through church leadership.
